## 2. Fearnleys / FearnResearch — weekly LNG rate history

**Send to:** the research or data contact listed on https://www.fearnleys.com
(and/or https://fearnpulse.com). Verify before sending.
**What makes this the most likely "yes":** you are asking for a historical copy of
something they already publish weekly, not for platform access.

> **Subject:** Academic data request — historical Fearnleys weekly LNG rates (TUM Bachelor thesis)
>
> Dear Fearnleys / FearnResearch team,
>
> I am an undergraduate student at the Technical University of Munich writing a
> Bachelor thesis in transportation analytics on LNG shipping around the 2026
> Strait of Hormuz disruption. The work is non-commercial academic research,
> supervised at TUM.
>
> The Fearnleys Weekly Report publishes the LNG rate table I need. I would like to
> ask whether FearnResearch can supply the historical series in a machine-readable
> file (CSV or XLSX).
>
> **Series requested — the four LNG figures from the weekly report:**
>
> - Spot, East of Suez, MEGI/XDF — USD/day
> - Spot, West of Suez, MEGI/XDF — USD/day
> - 1-year Time Charter, MEGI/XDF — USD/day
> - the corresponding weekly change values, if they are stored
>
> **Frequency:** weekly, as published.
> **Period:** from 2019-01-01, or the earliest available date, through the latest
> release. A pre-period covering at least 2022 onward is essential; the study's
> operational-onset date is 2026-02-28.
>
> **Two questions on methodology, which I would cite either way:**
>
> 1. Are the published rates broker assessments/estimates, or are they derived from
>    reported fixtures? I want to describe them accurately in the data chapter.
> 2. Has the vessel-class basis (MEGI/XDF) or the East/West of Suez definition
>    changed over the period? A definitional break would matter for the analysis.
>
> **Intended use.** I would compute and publish aggregated statistics, figures and
> model coefficients derived from the series, with full attribution to Fearnleys.
> I would **not** redistribute the raw weekly values — no rate table would appear
> in the thesis, its appendices, or any public repository. I would also ask
> permission to retain the file privately for the duration of the examination so my
> examiners can reproduce the results, and to delete it afterwards on request.
>
> I am happy to sign a non-commercial academic licence and to share the finished
> thesis.
>
> Thank you for considering this.
>
> Kind regards
> Mher Avetisyan · mher.avetisyan@tum.de · Technical University of Munich

## 3. Baltic Exchange — BLNG route history (optional third)

**Route:** start with the Free Trial form at
https://www.balticexchange.com/en/free-trial.html, then use the contact address at
https://www.balticexchange.com/en/contact-us.html for the licensing question.
**Why bother:** BLNG1/2/3-174 are the only publicly documented series that are
LNG-specific, route-specific *and* assessed at 174,000 cbm — the closest match to
the thesis specification outside Spark.

> **Subject:** Academic licence enquiry — BLNG route history for a TUM Bachelor thesis
>
> Dear Baltic Exchange team,
>
> I am writing a Bachelor thesis at the Technical University of Munich on LNG
> shipping around the 2026 Strait of Hormuz disruption. It is non-commercial
> academic research.
>
> I would like to ask about access to the LNG route assessments:
>
> - **BLNG1-174** Australia → Japan round voyage, 174,000 cbm
> - **BLNG2-174** US Gulf → Continent round voyage, 174,000 cbm
> - **BLNG3-174** US Gulf → Japan round voyage, 174,000 cbm
> - the 160,000 cbm `(g)` equivalents, if available, as a vessel-class sensitivity
>
> **Frequency:** daily, as assessed. **Period:** from the first publication date of
> each route through the latest available. The operational-onset date in the study
> is 2026-02-28, so continuous coverage across early 2026 is essential.
> **Delivery:** API or CSV export, with the methodology document and the route
> definitions.
>
> **Two questions:**
>
> 1. What is the first publication date for each `-174` route? I need to know how
>    long a pre-period is available.
> 2. Which licence covers academic use? I would publish only aggregated statistics
>    and model coefficients with attribution, and would not redistribute the
>    assessments themselves. I am unclear whether this falls under the Non-Display
>    Usage terms or requires a separate academic agreement.
>
> If a student or academic licence exists, I would be glad to hear the terms.
>
> Kind regards
> Mher Avetisyan · mher.avetisyan@tum.de · Technical University of Munich
