## 3. Contribution assessment — what this thesis actually shows

Ranked by scientific value × evidence strength. The work is well past "the
Strait closed"; the ranking below is the answer to that worry.

**#1 — The propagation finding: disruption moved into *source composition*, not
total volume.** (`AFFECTED_IMPORTER_FINDINGS.md`, basin exposure results.)
Importers defended aggregate LNG intake through substitution; the shock is
invisible in total-volume statistics and only appears when imports are
decomposed by origin — EU27 total +10% while Qatar-sourced → 0.0 in April;
Japan total +14% YoY while Gulf-sourced −27% YoY. Confirmed independently in
two official statistics (Eurostat, UN Comtrade) with no calibration, mirroring
the WTO/GFW cross-source agreement (−98.6%/−93.0%). **This is the fascinating,
usable insight**: exposure metrics based on total flows systematically
understate concentration risk, because adaptive capacity masks it. Currently
marked *preliminary/exploratory* — it needs promotion into a properly framed
descriptive results section. That is the highest-value writing task remaining.

**#2 — The transferable identification protocol (the methodology-first
headline).** Leakage-safe counterfactual forecasting + a falsification cascade
(placebo-in-time, 28-chokepoint placebo-in-space, donut treatment-date, leave-
one-donor-out, synthetic control, horizon-matched intervals, Romano-Wolf,
quantified dark-vessel bound) executed end-to-end on *entirely free data* with
hash-frozen reproducibility. Your own gap matrix shows no screened study
combines these elements. For a bachelor thesis this is the defensible
methodological contribution, and it is finished and verified.

**#3 — The quantified strait result.** −95.4% observed throughput; shortfall
5,121 transits [3,934–5,722] over 94 days; 3.9×–8.2× separation across four
independent designs; explicitly an upper bound with the critical dark-rate
computed. Necessary anchor, but on its own it would indeed be "the Strait
closed, quantified" — which is why #1 and #2 lead.

**#4 — The mechanism decomposition that *retires your own proposal's
headline*.** Voyages −23.4%, capacity-distance per retained voyage +10.2%
(BCa 4.4–17.0%), shift-share showing ~98% of that is route-composition, not
elongation. The honest conclusion — contraction + substitution, **no aggregate
ton-mile multiplier on free data** — is a genuine empirical correction to the
narrative the proposal assumed. Framed properly, disproving your own premise is
a strength examiners reward.

**#5 — Disciplined negative results.** The V1 coverage NO_GO (0/15 importers
admissible under the pre-registered rule) and the WTO rolling-window discovery
(P2) are both publishable-grade honesty artifacts; keep them in the thesis.
