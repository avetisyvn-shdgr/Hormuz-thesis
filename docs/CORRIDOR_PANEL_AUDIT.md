# Corridor-transmission panel audit

**Status:** Input panel frozen 2026-06-21; grouping and downstream thresholds
remain pending Prof. Li approval. No forecast or post-period deviation was
computed during this task.

## History policy

The panel uses `2022-01-01` through `2026-06-01` for every model. The PortWatch
snapshot begins in 2019, but the earlier history is intentionally excluded so
all candidate models receive the same context, the design matches the existing
validated benchmark, and the ~1,519-day pre-period remains within the locally
tested Moirai context. A model may not receive extra history merely because its
architecture permits it.

Training and validation remain strictly before `2026-02-28`. The existing
expanding 365-day/30-day-step policy produces 38 folds; the final validation
window ends `2026-02-13`.

## Operational grouping boundary

The `basin_group` field is an operational maritime-region label for map facets,
not a hydrological or origin/destination basin classification. Boundary routes
such as Suez, Gibraltar, Panama and Hormuz do not have a unique ocean-basin
assignment. The mapping therefore freezes `aggregation_allowed = false`.

Nine groups cover all 28 corridors. The largest is
`maritime_southeast_asia_oceania` with eight corridors. Group membership does
not imply traffic reallocation, and values must not be summed because a voyage
can cross multiple chokepoints in the same group.
